What to say in the room

Three angles, one per audience. Do not deliver all three to the same person — it reads as a feature tour instead of an answer.

  • Controls engineers, plant engineering, procurement — Angle 1. Hardware agnosticism. Deploy on any Linux or Windows device, no proprietary controller, no vendor-forced refresh cycle. This covers most of Ring 1.
  • IT/OT directors, digital transformation leads — Angle 2. Control runtime and data concentrator in one platform. Relevant at WSU, AGC, and Partner Therapeutics.
  • CIO/CTO, IT infrastructure — Angle 3. Control managed like an IT application: versioned, containerized, CI/CD capable. This is the PepsiCo and Lamb Weston enterprise conversation.

The wedge question works on anyone evaluating Siemens SIMATIC AX, Rockwell, Emerson, or Beckhoff: can you run this on a $400 industrial PC you already have on the plant floor? EAE can. Their platforms mostly cannot.

And keep the disqualifiers straight so you do not get caught out. EAE is not a SCADA replacement, not cloud-only, not Schneider-hardware-locked, and not a rip-and-replace mandate — it coexists with existing PLCs as a modernization and data concentration layer. In refrigeration and packaging retrofits like Tyson and Chukar, that coexistence is the pitch.
