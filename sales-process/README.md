# Sales Process

Scoring and cadence system for EAE business development conversations.

## Contents

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `CALL_SCORECARD_RUBRIC.md` | Pre-call gate, 10-dimension execution rubric, outcome grades, named anti-patterns, call log template |
| `CADENCE_TEMPLATES.md` | Four follow-up sequences by account archetype, plus partner-briefing and pre-meeting checklists |
| `calls/` | One scored log per conversation, `YYYY-MM-DD-account-slug.md` |

## Workflow

1. **Before the meeting** — score the Pre-Call Gate (Part A). It authorizes the meeting type and decides whether engineer time is justified.
2. **Within 24h** — score execution (Part B) from the transcript. Record the cadence checklist with real dates.
3. **When resolved** — grade the outcome (Part C).
4. **Monthly** — review the trailing 5 calls by *dimension average*, not composite. The composite hides which muscle is weak.

## Two tracks

Classify the **person**, not the building.

- **End User** — gate on trigger event, named pain, repeatability, budget proximity.
- **Integrator / Channel** — gate on scaling motive, segment fit, capability gap, willingness to learn. Their own facility's use case is irrelevant; their customer base is the opportunity.

Misclassifying an integrator as an end user makes a good account look like a bad one. See `calls/2026-08-12-astech-engineered-products.md` for a worked example.

## Notes

- Record the whole meeting, including plant-floor walks. The unstructured half is often where the real conversation happens.
- The gate exists to authorize saying no. A calendar full of low-gate meetings is what makes a seller lean, and buyers can feel leaning.
